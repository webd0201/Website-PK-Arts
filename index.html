Meet the Artist: Pawan (PK)

Pawan's gallery on Gokul Road has become a regular stop for people across Hubballi. Visitors come back because the work feels alive: every strand of hair, every shadow and every expression is drawn with patience and precision.

Hard work and dedication towards profession and passion.

That is how one client describes him, and it matches what many others say. From pencil and charcoal to acrylics and his signature blood art, Pawan treats each commission as something personal, made for one person and one story.
